# Quick Start Guide - For Everyone!

Welcome to the Training & Placement Portal! This guide will help you get started in 5 minutes, no technical knowledge needed.

## 🚀 What is This Portal?

The T&P Portal is a website where:

- **Students** find jobs and apply for placements
- **Recruiters** (companies) post job openings and hire students
- **Admins** manage everything and track statistics

## 📱 Getting Started (First Time Users)

### Step 1: Open the Website

```
http://localhost:3000
```

You'll see the home page with a login button.

### Step 2: Login or Register

- Click the **Login** button at the top
- OR click **Register** to create a new account

### Step 3: Choose Your Role

When registering, you'll see three options:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  I want to register as:                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ✓ Student (Looking for a job)             │
│ ✓ Recruiter (Hiring people)               │
│ ✓ Admin (Managing the portal)              │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Choose wisely!** Each role has different functions.

## 👨‍🎓 For Students

**What you can do:**

- View available job openings
- Apply to jobs that match your profile
- Upload your resume (PDF file)
- Track your applications
- See interview schedules
- Check job offers

**Your Dashboard shows:**

- Number of jobs available
- Your applications and their status
- Upcoming interviews
- Job offers you've received

### Your First Job Application

1. **Go to "Jobs"** - See all available positions
2. **Find a job** - Click on any job to see details
3. **Click "Apply"** - System checks if you're eligible
4. **Done!** - You'll see your application status

**What is "Eligibility"?**
The system checks if you meet the job requirements:

- Your CGPA (grades) must be high enough
- Your branch must be in the allowed list
- Your year must match what they need

If eligible ✅ - Your application is accepted
If not eligible ❌ - Application is auto-rejected (but you can try other jobs!)

### Tips for Students 💡

- **Upload your resume early** - Companies can see your skills
- **Apply to multiple jobs** - Increase your chances
- **Check regularly** - See updates on applications
- **Review offers carefully** - Before accepting a job

## 🏢 For Recruiters

**What you can do:**

- Post job openings
- View student applications
- Shortlist candidates
- Schedule interviews
- Review interview results
- Send job offers

**Your Dashboard shows:**

- Number of jobs posted
- Total applications received
- Selected candidates
- Offers sent

### Posting Your First Job

1. **Go to "Post a Job"**
2. **Fill in the details:**
   - Job Title (e.g., "Software Engineer")
   - Description (what the job is about)
   - Location
   - Salary (in lakhs)
   - Minimum CGPA required
   - Allowed branches (CSE, IT, etc.)
   - Allowed years (3rd year, 4th year)

3. **Click "Post Job"** - Students will see it!

### Managing Applications

1. **Go to "Applications"**
2. **See all students who applied**
3. **View their resume and match score** (0-100 eligibility %)
4. **Actions:**
   - ✅ **Shortlist** - Candidate moves to interview stage
   - ❌ **Reject** - Candidate is not selected
   - 📋 **Check Resume** - See their skills and education

### Interview Process

1. **Schedule Interview**
   - Pick students to interview
   - Choose interview date, time, location
   - Add interview type (Technical, HR, etc.)

2. **Record Results**
   - After interview, update result (Passed/Failed)
   - Add feedback and score

3. **Send Offer**
   - For selected candidates, generate offer letter
   - Specify final salary (CTC)
   - Student receives notification

### Tips for Recruiters 💡

- **Write clear job descriptions** - Students understand better
- **Review resumes carefully** - Look at extracted skills
- **Schedule interviews promptly** - Don't delay results
- **Track your pipeline** - Use dashboard analytics
- **Communicate with students** - Clear feedback help

## 🎯 Common Questions

### Q: Forgot your password?

A: Use "Forgot Password" on login page. Enter your email and you'll get reset instructions.

### Q: Can I change my profile details?

A: Yes! Go to "Profile" and update your information.

### Q: Can I withdraw an application?

A: Once shortlisted, it's harder. Contact admin for help.

### Q: How do I know if my resume was parsed correctly?

A: Go to "My Resume" and see:

- Extracted skills
- Education details
- Detected CGPA
- Keywords for job matching

### Q: What does "Match Score" mean?

A: It's a percentage (0-100) showing how well your resume matches the job requirements. Higher is better!

### Q: Can recruiters see my resume?

A: Only after you apply to their job. They see:

- Your extracted skills
- Education
- Parsed CGPA
- Match score for their job

### Q: When do I get the job offer?

A: Steps are:

1. Apply to job ➜
2. Recruiter shortlists you ➜
3. Interview scheduled ➜
4. Interview results ➜
5. If selected, offer sent ➜
6. You accept/decline

### Q: Is this website safe?

A: Yes! Your password is encrypted. We track all activities. Only authorized people see your data.

## 🔐 Security Tips

✅ **DO:**

- Use a strong password (mix of numbers, letters, symbols)
- Never share your login credentials
- Log out when done (especially on shared computers)
- Review emails from the portal
- Check your profile regularly

❌ **DON'T:**

- Use simple passwords (12345, your name)
- Share password with friends
- Leave computer logged in unattended
- Click suspicious links
- Trust unsolicited emails
